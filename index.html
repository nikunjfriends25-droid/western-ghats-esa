Reference layers

Kerala publishes its own ESA as KML, defined as village portions. 98.1% of that official area falls inside these whole-village polygons — so the blue outline should sit within the purple, and the purple extends beyond it. The reverse figure is 66.7%, because a whole village is larger than the portion of it that was notified.
